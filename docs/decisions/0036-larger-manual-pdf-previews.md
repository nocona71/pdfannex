# Use larger manual PDF previews

## Context

The initial three-column preview grid fit all five showcase pages on one manual
page, but the reduced pages were too small to inspect.

## Decision

Show the preview pages in two columns at a larger scale. Place the first four
pages on one manual page and the fifth page with the following snippets.

## Consequences

Readers can inspect the sample content more easily while the manual remains
portrait-oriented. The gallery takes more space and may add a page to the
manual.
